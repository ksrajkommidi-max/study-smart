👋 Quick start — how to use this website

1. Choose a subject and chapter in Google Research Desk. 2. Open a lesson or take a quiz. 3. Save your notes and check your progress. 4. Use Settings to change colours. Classroom controls are demonstrations only; they do not operate real equipment.

Find study resources by subject and chapter. Search results open in a new Google or YouTube tab.
